What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links on Zheoa are affiliate links — special URLs that let the retailer know you came from us. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission.

📌 The short version: You never pay more. We earn a small commission from the retailer if you buy something.

Our Affiliate Partners

We currently work with:

🛒 Amazon Associates

🌐 Impact.com
Brand partners across fashion. Links to brand stores in supported regions.

We only link to products and brands we believe are genuinely worth considering.

How It Works

When you click a Zheoa link:

  • Our system detects your country (US, UK, IT, IN, or default).
  • It resolves the best affiliate URL for that region.
  • You are redirected to the retailer's website.
  • If you make a purchase, the retailer pays us a small commission — you pay the same price you would pay going there directly.

Editorial Independence

Our recommendations are independent. We:

  • Do not accept payment for positive reviews.
  • Do not guarantee that a specific product will suit you — fashion is personal.
  • Do not control pricing, availability, shipping, or returns — those are handled by the retailer.

Your Responsibility

Before buying anything we link to:

  • Verify the product details, sizing, and materials on the seller's site.
  • Check current prices, availability and shipping options.
  • Read the seller's return, refund and warranty policies.

Zheoa does not handle any purchase, payment, or post-sale issue. All such matters are between you and the retailer.

Pricing & Commission

  • No extra cost to you. The price you pay is what the seller charges.
  • Commission rates vary by program and product — typically 1–10% of the sale.
  • Not every click earns us anything. Only completed purchases generate commissions.
