1. Information We Collect

Zheoa is a fashion discovery and outfit-combo website. We do not sell clothing, do not process payments, and do not handle shipping or returns. We collect limited information to operate the site and improve your experience.

Automatically collected

  • Visitor ID — a random identifier stored in your browser's local storage to distinguish unique visitors for click analytics.
  • Country code — detected from your IP address to show region-appropriate affiliate links. We cache this for 1 hour locally.
  • IP address — logged with affiliate clicks for fraud prevention and analytics.
  • Browser and device — user agent string logged with clicks.
  • Click events — which affiliate links you click, and which article or product they came from.

Voluntarily provided

  • Email — only if you contact us or if we add an admin login for you.

📌 We don't collect: your name, phone number, postal address, payment details, or sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use It

  • Analytics — understanding which articles, products, and combos are popular.
  • Personalization — showing affiliate links that work in your country.
  • Fraud prevention — rate-limiting abusive click patterns.
  • Communication — responding if you email us.

We process data under the following legal bases (GDPR):

  • Consent — for storing non-essential data (visitor ID, country cache).
  • Legitimate interest — for click analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation — where required by law.

3. Local Storage

Zheoa uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to remember:

  • visitorId — your unique visitor identifier
  • userCountry — your detected country code
  • userCountryTimestamp — cache expiry for country detection
  • cookiesAccepted — your consent preference
  • affiliate_links_* — cached affiliate link mappings (1-hour expiry)

You can clear all localStorage data at any time via your browser settings. Once cleared, the site will re-request consent.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider. Stores click logs, visitor IDs, and admin credentials. Privacy policy.
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate partner. When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may place cookies for conversion tracking. Amazon privacy.
  • Impact.com — affiliate network for brand partners. May set cookies for attribution. Impact privacy.
  • IP geolocation APIs (ipapi.co, ip-api.com, ipinfo.io) — used to detect your country. No persistent cookies.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Inter typeface. Your IP is transmitted to Google. Google privacy.
  • Pexels — image hosting for product/editorial content. No personal data collected.

Each provider operates under its own privacy policy. We do not control the data they collect.

5. Data Retention

  • Click logs — retained for 24 months, then anonymized.
  • Visitor ID — stored in your browser indefinitely until you clear it.
  • Admin accounts — retained while active; deleted on request.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectify — correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete — request erasure of your data.
  • Restrict — limit how we use your data.
  • Port — receive your data in machine-readable form.
  • Object — object to legitimate-interest processing.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time (clearing localStorage achieves this).

California (CCPA): we do not sell personal data.

India (DPDP Act 2023): Grievance officer contact below.

To exercise any right, email privacy@zheoa.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Zheoa is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Contact

  • 📧 Email: privacy@zheoa.com
  • 🏢 Grievance officer (India): grievance@zheoa.com

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on the site and the "Last updated" date revised.
