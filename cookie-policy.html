🍪 Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

What Zheoa stores in your browser and why.

Cookies vs. Local Storage

Cookies are small text files sent with every HTTP request. Local storage is browser-side storage that isn't automatically sent to our servers.

Zheoa primarily uses localStorage rather than traditional cookies. This reduces the amount of data that leaves your browser.

📌 Key point: We store minimal data locally, and none of it identifies you personally.

What We Store

Key Purpose Type Expiry
cookiesAccepted Your consent preference (true / false). Essential Persistent
visitorId Random ID to distinguish you from other visitors for analytics. Functional Persistent
userCountry Detected country code for affiliate link resolution. Functional 1 hour (cached)
userCountryTimestamp When country was last detected. Functional 1 hour
affiliate_links_* Cached affiliate link mappings per product/article. Performance 1 hour

We do NOT use:

  • Third-party advertising cookies
  • Social media pixels (Facebook, TikTok, etc.)
  • Session recording or heatmap tools

Third-Party Cookies

When you click certain links or load certain assets, these third parties may set cookies on your device:

  • Amazon Associates — conversion tracking if you click Amazon links.
  • Impact.com — affiliate conversion tracking (loaded only after consent).
  • Supabase — session cookies if you log in as an admin.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter typeface. No cookies, but your IP is transmitted to Google.

We do not control these cookies. Review each provider's own policy for details.

Managing Your Preferences

Accept / Decline

On first visit, a banner asks you to accept or decline non-essential storage. Your choice is stored and respected.

Clear Local Storage

You can clear all stored data at any time via your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Clear browsing data.

If you decline, only essential consent preference is stored. Some features (personalized affiliate links) may not work optimally.

Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in technology or law. The "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly. Material changes will be announced on the site.
